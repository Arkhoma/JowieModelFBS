2026–27 format. Auto bids: the ACC, Big Ten, Big 12 and SEC champions (however they rank), the highest-ranked team from the American, Conference USA, MAC, Mountain West, Pac-12 and Sun Belt (it needn't be a champion), and Notre Dame if it ranks in the top 12 — which leaves 6 at-large spots instead of 7. Seeding is straight by ranking: the four best-ranked teams get byes, and an auto bid ranked outside the top 12 sits at the bottom. Rankings are the Resume ranking, re-run for each simulated season, and "champion" means the best in-conference record in that season — so a wide-open race sends different teams in different seasons. The bracket above is the 12 teams most often in the field; the four likeliest byes take seeds 1–4 and the rest are ordered by the average seed they got when they made it. The odds count every simulated season, including the ones where someone not shown here gets in. Older seasons are what-ifs under these rules.
